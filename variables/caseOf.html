      • caseOf is the case analysis of a sum type — a case-of over a discriminated union. Name the field that discriminates, give an arm per case you care about, and each arm receives the member already narrowed. The result rides a Maybe: an unmatched case is Nothing, so the miss stays the caller's decision. either is its two-branch special case on the shipped containers; matchOn is its value-level sibling.

        type Shape = {kind: 'circle'; radius: number} | {kind: 'square'; side: number};

        caseOf('kind')(shape, {
            circle: ({radius}) => Math.PI * radius * radius,
            square: ({side}) => side * side
        }).orElse(0);
